## What this is

An entry for the make.fun bounty "The Dog Reviews the Mailman": one funny
one-line review that a dog gives the mailman, under 200 characters.

`main.py` (Python stdlib only) writes a single line to `entry.txt` in the
output directory read from `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (falling back to `/output`).
The line is a one-star review from the dog: the mailman comes every day,
teases him through the fence, and has never once brought a treat — yet after
three years of barking, the dog is somehow the rude one.
